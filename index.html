FIRST Tech Challenge

Learn About FTC

FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) is a high school robotics program where students design, build, and code robots to compete in global engineering challenges. Our FTC teams thrive on collaboration and innovation, competing at regional, provincial, and world championship levels each season.

FIRST LEGO League

Learn About FLL

FIRST LEGO League (FLL) introduces younger students to STEM through hands-on challenges using LEGO robots. It fosters critical thinking, teamwork, and creativity in a fun, engaging environment. Renert's five FLL teams compete annually and regularly advance to provincial championships, inspiring the next generation of roboticists.
