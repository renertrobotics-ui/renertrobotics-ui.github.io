Octopi Robotics

Renert School • Calgary, Alberta • Founded September 2023

Team Foundation

Octopi Robotics was founded in September 2023 and became Renert School’s official FIRST Tech Challenge team in 2024. The team was established to build a sustainable, competitive robotics program focused on long-term growth, mentorship, and technical excellence.

We compete in the FIRST Tech Challenge program, representing Calgary, Alberta at the provincial level and beyond.

Program Development & Sustainability

Octopi Robotics operates under a structured development model emphasizing offseason training, knowledge transfer between members, and continuous skill progression.

  • Structured preseason technical workshops
  • Cross-training across engineering and programming disciplines
  • Documented processes for long-term team continuity
  • Strategic planning for multi-season improvement

Community Engagement

Our team is committed to expanding STEM access within our school and broader community. We actively promote robotics education, mentorship, and participation in competitive STEM programs.

  • STEM advocacy within Renert School
  • Mentorship of newer robotics students
  • Promotion of FIRST values and gracious professionalism
