Engineering The Future.
One Robot At A Time.

FTC Team 27361 Octopi Robotics from Calgary is a passionate, driven group of innovators based at Renert School. We combine creativity, precision engineering, and teamwork to compete at the highest level in the FIRST Tech Challenge.

Who We Are

Octopi Robotics is more than just a robotics team — we are problem-solvers, leaders, programmers, builders, and strategists. Our mission is to inspire innovation, push engineering boundaries, and develop real-world skills through competitive robotics.

Each season, we design, prototype, manufacture, and program a competition-ready robot from scratch. Through countless hours of testing and iteration, we refine our machine to perform with precision under pressure.

Our Core Values

Innovation

We embrace creativity and engineering excellence to design advanced, competitive robotics solutions.

Teamwork

Collaboration drives our success. Every member plays a critical role in our journey.

Leadership

We strive to inspire our community and mentor younger students in STEM and robotics.

What We're Building

We are currently developing our 2025 competition robot — engineered for speed, precision, and adaptability. Our design focuses on mechanical efficiency, robust programming, and strategic gameplay execution.

Follow our journey as we refine our systems and prepare to compete at regionals and beyond.

Meet The Team

Behind every successful robot is a dedicated team of students working tirelessly behind the scenes. From CAD designers to programmers, outreach leads to mechanical engineers — every member contributes to our success.
