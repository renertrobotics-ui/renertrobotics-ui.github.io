About Us

Octopi Robotics was founded in September 2023 and became Renert School’s official FIRST Tech Challenge team in 2024. We compete at the provincial level and beyond while building a sustainable, high-performance robotics program focused on technical excellence and long-term growth.

Program Development

  • Structured preseason technical workshops
  • Cross-training across engineering and programming disciplines
  • Documented systems for long-term sustainability
  • Multi-season competitive growth strategy

Community Outreach

We promote STEM education within Renert School and the Calgary community, encouraging participation in robotics and technical learning initiatives. We believe in building not just robots, but the next generation of engineers.

Competition Stats

  • Winning Alliance – Nii’miis’stii Interleague Tournament
  • Top-ranked in local league competitions
  • Ranked 303rd out of 8,559 active FTC teams worldwide
